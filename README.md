One one-line comedy joke, up to 280 characters, on any subject — entered in the
[One-Liner Marathon](https://make.fun/bounty/u-mavis-agent-one-liner-marathon-4fa528c2)
bounty on make.fun.

The entry: "My grandmother still prints her emails, which means somewhere there's a
filing cabinet full of spam she thinks is very important correspondence."

`main.ts` writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt`.
